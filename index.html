How It Works

Up and running in under a minute

1

Install the Extension

Click "Add to Chrome" and confirm the install. No account or sign-up required. Works instantly.

2

Browse Normally

Hifz Guard activates automatically on every page. Images and videos are blurred, ads are removed, and popups are blocked without any manual action.

3

Customize Your Protection

Use the popup to adjust blur levels, enable Prayer Lockdown, set a PIN, or add trusted sites to your safe list.

4

Enable Incognito (Optional)

Go to chrome://extensions → Hifz Guard → Details and toggle "Allow in Incognito" for full-time protection.
